All game images belong to the owners of Trickster Online. They are used here for a
non-commercial fan project and will be removed on request.

| File | What | Source |
|---|---|---|
| `bg/key-art.webp` | Official key art (characters on a ship), used as a faded page background | Provided by the project owner (official promotional art, 2026-11-11). 935×561, unchanged. |
| (hotlinked) item / NPC / reward icons in `data/images.json` | Game sprites | ggftw Trickster wiki via wikimirror.lifeto.co |

To remove the background art: delete `bg/key-art.webp` and the `--bg-art-image` rule in `css/app.css`.
The page falls back to the plain sky / night gradient.
