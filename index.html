Stage 1: Coral Beach & Episode 0 Chapter 1 (Levels 1 -> 13)

Start in Coral Town - Blooming Cora with Heidi - Trickster Registration to get your Rookie gear. Follow the 10-step chain through Bunny Maid, Winnie, Lifeguard Deen (Field 1), Tinnie (Field 2), and Lifeguard Bean (Field 3) to earn your Young Egg pet and Pocket Pouch. At Level 12, pick up the Field 3 & Dark Cave side/monster quests (Explorer Reina, Monkey T, Hunter Yuri, Warrior Kei, Assistant Hunter) before heading through Dark Cave to Gate of Desert Beach.

2

Stage 2: Episode 0 Chapter 2 - Core Mechanics Tutorials (Levels 13 -> 20)

At Gate of Desert Beach, speak to Don Giuvanni and Driller Marky to drill 2x Tanning Oil and earn your Peng pet. Continue to Beach Town - Paradise and complete Officer Robert's 3 stages with Compounder Paul (Desert Potion), Alchemist Nate (Life Vest + Hard Scale compounding), and Blacksmith Marx (drill 7x Bronze Fragment in Crystal Copper Mine and refine your Wooden Sword to Lv.1).

3

Stage 3: Episode 0 Chapter 3 - Andrew & The 8 Heart Gems (Levels 20 -> 25)

At Level 20+, talk to Andrew in Paradise to start Meet the Natives! Complete all 8 resident quests (Steve Ryu, Don Giuvanni, Clever Owl, Kristoffer J., Dorothy, Officer Tera, Old Artist in Field 2, and Rosemary). Your own character class's NPC will give you a special class quest that rewards a Class Guardian Pet + Stat Ring! Turn all 8x Heart Gem in to Andrew to finish Episode 0 and receive the Health Charm.

4

Stage 4: Desert Beach Key Quests & 5 Desert Stickers (Levels 25 -> 32)

At Level 25+, talk to Sandman in Paradise. Complete any 5 of the 7 Desert Sticker quests (5 cycles each). Pro Tip: Homeless Ian gives Water Buckets which feed directly into Bunny Maid, which gives Filled Water Buckets that feed directly into Al-Hauri - doing those 3 together plus Miranda Watty (10x Pineapple Mask) and Vagabond Eloy (10x Sun Cream) or Skipper Min is the fastest 5-sticker route! Then complete Legendary Recipe and The Miracle of Sand to earn the Adventurer's Book and Weapon Ticket A.

5

Stage 5: Megalopolis & Poppuri Dungeon Key Quests (Levels 30 -> 45)

Follow Sandman's breadcrumb to Guild Clerk Esther in Megalopolis Guild Office, then meet Scared Poppuri in Southeast Forest. Drill the Toy Box, hunt the 4 missing toys (Simbatta, Sppo, Moss Moth, Larva), and earn 4x Instant Adhesive from any 4 of the 5 Poppuri sub-quest chains for Artisan Poppuri. Deliver the Mended Toy Box to Stern Poppuri in Slide Cave to complete Poppuri Redemption. Also complete Indiana John - Old Man's Dream in Southeast Forest to unlock Surprise Spots across Caballa Island!

6

Stage 6: Episode 1 Chapter 1 - Nefertiti's Necklace (Levels 45 -> 50)

At Level 45+, head to Gate of Caballa Relics and give 5x 50 Galder Coupon to Monkey T for the Faded Necklace. Speak to Old Artist in Relics Field 1 (during in-game daytime!) with Ginseng Tea, fill the container via Guide Sabrina in Azteca (5x Gold + 1x Golden Egg), and complete the mirror sub-quest chain with Model Mermaid Babe, Monkey T, and Louis Bitton. Take the Restored Necklace to Love Hunter Robin (Path to Caballa Relics) and finish Chapter 1 at Rosaline Gracia in Megalopolis Square with Honey Tea!

7

Stage 7: Episode 1 Chapters 2 & 3 - Unknown Ancient Box & Harkon Searchers (Levels 50 -> 55+)

At Level 50+, enter Caballa Relics Dungeon 1 and speak to Indiana John. Gather the 3 Weird Pieces in Oops Wharf from Skipper Min, Shaman Girl Jia, and Vagabond Eloy. Unlock the Weird Box in Relics Dungeon 1 using the password "Vendetta" and hunt 2x Lamplight from Naranjo. At Level 52+, start Chapter 3 with Tango at Gate of Oops Wharf, help Explorer Reina in Path to Mermaid Palace 1, and compound Transparent Medicine for Fairy Nono in Path to Mermaid Palace 2 to complete Episode 1 and earn the Nono Ring!

8

Stage 8: Caballa Relics Key Quests (10 Caballa Stickers) & 2nd Job Advancement (Levels 55 -> 65)

In Relics Town - Azteca, collect any 10 of the 13 Caballa Stickers for Fortune Teller (tip: completing the 4 Dr. Cat quests + Genius Cochma gives 5 stickers at once, and Fairy Feria + Guide Sabrina's Catch That Thief! gives 2 stickers for 15x Patterned/Plain Pottery!). Recover Keeper Julio's Storage Key from Lima, remember to show the Gold Silk Pouch to Fortune Teller FIRST, and then return it to Keeper Julio for Weapon Ticket B. Finally, hunt Kaboom (Lv.60) in Relics Dungeon 4 for a Growth Badge + buy 3x 100k Galder Check from Andrew to complete your 2nd Job Advancement in Azteca!
